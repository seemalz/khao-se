Our Story

Born from Passion,
Served with Love

Khao Se was founded by Jojo and Nabiha — two friends with a shared obsession for bold, aromatic flavours rooted in Southeast Asian tradition. What started as cooking for friends quickly became Toronto's most talked-about catering experience.

Every dish is a conversation between heritage and creativity. We source locally, cook from scratch, and believe that food should do more than fill you up — it should move you.
