Our Story

Two Friends.
One Kitchen.
Endless Flavour.

Jojo and Nabiha started Khao Se To the way all great things start — feeding their friends. What began as weekend cookouts and potluck takeovers turned into something bigger: a catering company built on the belief that food should make people feel something.

Rooted in Southeast Asian tradition and shaped by Toronto's melting pot, every dish tells a story — of late-night recipe experiments, of grandmothers' kitchens, of markets in Bangkok and Yangon and Kuala Lumpur. This is food that remembers where it came from.

"We don't just cater events — we create the moment everyone gathers around."

Toronto is one of the most diverse food cities on the planet — and Khao Se To is a product of that energy. We source from local farms and markets, collaborate with Toronto's creative community, and bring our food to neighbourhoods across the GTA.

From Kensington Market pop-ups to Yorkville dinner parties, Scarborough birthdays to downtown corporate events — wherever the city gathers, we'll be there.
